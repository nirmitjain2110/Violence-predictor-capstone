Section 5: Experimental Results, Temporal Escalation Analysis, and Discussion

Executive Summary: This section presents a comprehensive evaluation of our proposed predictive spatio-temporal violence framework across four benchmark datasets. Rather than treating violence detection as a static, reactive classification task, our system evaluates progressive temporal probability trajectories P(t) across sequential prefix windows. The model reliably identifies violent escalation with an average early-warning lead time of 3.48 seconds prior to peak incident severity.

5.1 Benchmark Datasets & Evaluation Protocol

To rigorously evaluate every module of the framework without methodological disconnect, experiments are structured across four specialized datasets:

5.2 Spatial-Temporal Violence Recognition on Fixed Benchmarks (RWF-2000 & RLVS)

We first benchmark the base spatio-temporal video encoder (MobileNetV3/ResNet-18 + Bi-GRU) and the acoustic anomaly stream (Mel-spectrogram Conv2D + BiLSTM) on pre-trimmed binary benchmarks.

5.2.1 Mathematical Formulation of Base Subsystems

Spatial Feature Extraction:
ft = φspatial(It) ∈ ℝ512,    for t ∈ {1, 2, …, 16}

αt = exp(waT tanh(Wh ht + bh)) / ∑k=116 exp(waT tanh(Wh hk + bh))

aembed = BiLSTM(Conv2D(Mmel)) ∈ ℝ128
afinal = maudio · aembed + (1 - maudio) · efallback

5.4 Spatio-Temporal Escalation & Early-Warning Forecasting (XD-Violence)

Real-world surveillance requires forecasting threat progression in real time. Using the untrimmed XD-Violence benchmark (709 held-out test videos: 341 violent, 368 normal), we evaluate progressive partial-window evaluation across 16 sequential steps.

5.4.1 Mathematical Formulation of Predictive Trajectory Metrics

(1) Progressive Prefix Inference:
P(t) = σ(Wcls · ht(1:t) + bcls) = 1 / (1 + exp(-(Wcls · ht(1:t) + bcls))),    ∀ t ∈ {1, 2, …, 16}
(2) Threshold-Crossing Timestep:
tcross = min { t ∈ {1, …, 16} | P(t) ≥ τ* }
(3) Operational Lead Time (Δtlead):
Δtlead = (16 - tcross) × 0.25 s
(4) Escalation Rate (Slope S):
S = (P̄late - P̄early) / 12 = [(∑t=1216 P(t) / 5) - (∑t=15 P(t) / 5)] / 12
(5) Fused Predictive Probability:
P(Vt+Δt) = σ(Wpred · Ffusion + bpred)
